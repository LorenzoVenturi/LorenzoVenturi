# Hi, I'm Lorenzo

I'm an Artificial Intelligence graduate with interests in natural language processing, computer vision, and AI agents.

I completed a research internship at the **National Institute of Informatics (NII) in Tokyo**, where I worked on **visual misinformation on social media and Community Notes**.

I'm currently deepening my understanding of **AI agents and agentic workflows** through hands-on projects. Here I share work from my studies, research, and ongoing experiments.

## What I'm building now

### [Agentic Paper Retriever](https://github.com/LorenzoVenturi/agentic-paper-retrieval)

A local workspace for finding research papers with LangGraph and Ollama. It helps refine a question, searches arXiv with your approval, and uses the results to decide what to explore next.
